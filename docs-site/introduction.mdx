## Why Arc Insights

Most BI tools force a trade-off: easy-to-use but closed-source (Looker, Tableau), open-source but operationally heavy (Superset), or modern but locked to one cloud (Sigma, Hex). **Arc Insights is the alternative** — modern, open-source, and built from day one to ship to other people's servers, including air-gapped ones.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Single-binary deployment" icon="cube">
    A Bun-compiled binary or distroless Docker image. No JVM, no Celery, no
    Redis required for small deploys.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Embed-first SDKs" icon="code">
    Eden Treaty SDK gives clients fully typed React, Vue, and vanilla
    components. iframe support for fast integration.
  </Card>
  <Card title="On-prem & air-gapped" icon="lock">
    Helm chart, BYOK / CMEK, license-key activation, and a tested air-gapped
    install bundle.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bring-your-own everything" icon="server">
    Your LLM, your KMS, your Postgres, your warehouse. Defaults work for cloud
    customers; locals work for everyone else.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Where to start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the dev loop">
    `git clone` the repo, `bun install`, `make dev`. Five-minute setup; see the
    [Quickstart](/quickstart).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a database">
    Postgres, MySQL, Snowflake, BigQuery — pick a connector and we'll scan the
    schema for you. (Phase 1 work — drivers landing M1–M2.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a dashboard">
    Visual no-code builder or raw SQL. Apache ECharts and AG Grid render the
    output. (Phase 1.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Embed it">
    Sign a JWT, drop in our React/Vue SDK, and you're live in your customer's
    product. (Phase 3.)
  </Step>
</Steps>
